Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the private tool used by the owner of the Gravin YouTube channel. The tool runs only on the owner's own computer.

What the app does

  • It uploads the owner's own Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API v3 (video file, title, description, tags and thumbnail).
  • It reads the owner's own channel statistics (views, watch time and similar) through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. This access is read-only.

What data is used

The app only works with the owner's own Google account and YouTube channel. It does not access anyone else's account or data, and it does not store any data about other people.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are kept only on the owner's computer. We do not sell, share or transfer data to anyone. There are no ads and no tracking, and these web pages use no cookies.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services and follows the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Contact

You can reach the owner through the Gravin YouTube channel.
